Reinforcement learning · 2026

TD3 AutoNav: learned maze navigation

TurtleBot3 · Gazebo · ROS2 Humble · Stable-Baselines3

A TD3 agent that navigates mazes from 2D LiDAR scans and relative-goal observations, trained with BFS geodesic reward shaping to avoid sparse-reward stagnation. Trained on a single maze, it transfers to unseen layouts without retraining and reaches ~98% success on the training map.
